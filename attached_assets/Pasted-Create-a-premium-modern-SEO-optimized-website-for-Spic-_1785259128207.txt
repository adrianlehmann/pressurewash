Company Description

Spic & Span Pros is a locally owned, family-operated exterior cleaning company dedicated to delivering exceptional workmanship with honesty, respect, and outstanding customer service.

Since 2017, the company has helped homeowners restore and protect their properties through professional power washing, soft washing, window cleaning, gutter cleaning, and solar panel cleaning.

Their mission is simple:

Provide 5-star service, outstanding communication, fair pricing, and results that exceed expectations while treating every customer's home as if it were their own.

About the Owner

Brian Ochoa is the owner of Spic & Span Pros.

A former firefighter, Brian brings the same discipline, professionalism, attention to safety, and strong work ethic to every project.

Having grown up in Piscataway, he takes pride in helping homeowners throughout his community maintain beautiful homes.

His philosophy is simple:

Treat customers with honesty and respect while delivering exceptional quality every single time.
